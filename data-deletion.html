User Data Deletion Instructions

Student Retention Tracking and Reporting System

Users may request deletion of personal information processed by the Student Retention Tracking and Reporting System.

How to Request Data Deletion

To request deletion of your information, contact the administrator of the Student Retention Tracking and Reporting System.

Please provide enough information to identify your record, such as your name, student number, email address, or telephone number.

Information That May Be Deleted

  • Student contact information
  • WhatsApp communication records
  • Email or SMS communication records
  • Student responses
  • Retention case communication history
  • Other personal information associated with the prototype

Some academic, registration, or institutional records may need to be retained where required by institutional policy or applicable law.

Processing a Deletion Request

Once a valid deletion request is received, the administrator will verify the request and determine which information can be deleted.

Where deletion is permitted, the information will be removed from the Student Retention Tracking and Reporting System.
