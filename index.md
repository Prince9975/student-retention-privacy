# Privacy Policy

## Student Retention Tracking and Reporting System

**Effective Date:** 26 October 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how the Student Retention Tracking and Reporting System collects, uses, stores, and protects information processed by the system.

## 1. Purpose of the System

The Student Retention Tracking and Reporting System is an academic prototype designed to assist higher education institutions in identifying, monitoring, and following up students who have not returned or registered for an academic period.

The system may use communication channels such as WhatsApp, email, and SMS to contact eligible students and support student-retention follow-up activities.

## 2. Information We Process

The system may process information including:

- Student name
- Student number or registration number
- Email address
- Telephone or WhatsApp number
- Academic period information
- Registration status
- Retention case information
- Messages sent to students
- Responses received from students
- Communication history
- Follow-up actions recorded by authorized staff

## 3. How Information Is Used

Information is used for student-retention purposes, including:

- Identifying students who have not registered after an academic registration deadline
- Contacting eligible students
- Recording communications with students
- Monitoring student-retention cases
- Supporting follow-up activities
- Escalating difficult cases to authorized staff
- Generating retention reports
- Evaluating the academic prototype

## 4. WhatsApp Communication

Where WhatsApp is used, the system may process the student's WhatsApp number, messages sent by the system, student responses, and message-delivery information.

WhatsApp communication is provided through the WhatsApp Business Platform operated by Meta.

WhatsApp information is not used by this system for advertising or unrelated commercial purposes.

## 5. Artificial Intelligence

The prototype may use artificial intelligence to assist with responding to student messages.

AI-generated responses are intended to support student-retention communication.

Cases requiring human intervention may be escalated to authorized Student Affairs staff.

Important institutional decisions are not intended to be made solely by the AI component.

## 6. Access to Information

Information within the system is intended to be accessed only by authorized users according to their assigned roles.

Authorized users may include:

- System administrators
- Student Affairs staff
- Retention managers

## 7. Data Protection

Reasonable technical and administrative controls are used to protect information from unauthorized access, disclosure, modification, or misuse.

These controls may include authentication, role-based access control, database protection, and communication-provider security mechanisms.

## 8. Data Sharing

Personal information is not intended to be sold to third parties.

Information may be transmitted to communication providers where required to send or receive WhatsApp, email, or SMS communications.

These providers may process information according to their own privacy policies and service terms.

## 9. Data Retention

Information may be retained for the period necessary to support student-retention follow-up, system evaluation, academic research, and applicable institutional requirements.

Prototype or test information may be removed when it is no longer required.

## 10. User Rights

Where applicable, users may request:

- Access to information held about them
- Correction of inaccurate information
- Information about how their data is being used
- Deletion of information where deletion is permitted by institutional or legal requirements

Requests should be directed to the person or institution responsible for operating the Student Retention Tracking and Reporting System.

## 11. Academic Prototype

The Student Retention Tracking and Reporting System is currently being developed and evaluated as an academic prototype.

Its features, integrations, and data-processing procedures may change during development and testing.

## 12. Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated when the system's functionality, communication services, or data-processing practices change.

The latest version will be published on this page.
